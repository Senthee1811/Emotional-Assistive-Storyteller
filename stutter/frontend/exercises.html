Speech Therapy Exercises

Practice these exercises to improve speech fluency

Mild Exercises

MILD

Slow Reading Practice

Read a paragraph at half your normal speaking speed, focusing on each word. This helps improve rhythm and reduce rushing.

Instructions:
  1. Choose a simple paragraph or children's book
  2. Read each word slowly and clearly
  3. Take brief pauses between sentences
  4. Focus on proper pronunciation
  5. Practice for 5-10 minutes daily
MILD

Deep Breathing Exercise

Proper breathing technique reduces anxiety and improves speech control. Practice diaphragmatic breathing for better voice support.

Instructions:
  1. Sit comfortably with straight posture
  2. Inhale deeply through your nose for 4 counts
  3. Hold your breath for 4 counts
  4. Exhale slowly through your mouth for 6 counts
  5. Repeat 10-15 times
  6. Practice twice daily
MILD

Gentle Onset Technique

Start words with a soft, gentle sound instead of forcing them out abruptly. This reduces speech tension and improves flow.

Instructions:
  1. Choose simple words like "cat", "dog", "sun"
  2. Practice saying the word with minimal lip pressure
  3. Start with a gentle "h" or "sh" sound
  4. Gradually increase to the full word
  5. Practice 5-10 minutes daily
MILD

Pausing Technique

Insert brief pauses between phrases to reduce rushing and anxiety. This gives you time to plan your next words and speak more calmly.

Instructions:
  1. Choose short phrases or sentences
  2. Practice saying a phrase, then pause for 2-3 seconds
  3. Focus on relaxing your jaw and shoulders
  4. Gradually increase phrase length
  5. Use in conversation practice
MILD

Mirror Practice

Watch yourself speak in a mirror to identify tension in facial muscles. This awareness helps you relax and speak more naturally.

Instructions:
  1. Stand in front of a mirror at eye level
  2. Practice saying simple words and phrases
  3. Watch for tension in your forehead, jaw, and neck
  4. Relax tense muscles when you notice them
  5. Practice 5-10 minutes daily
MILD

Syllable Repetition

Practice saying multi-syllable words slowly, breaking them into parts. This improves control over speech rhythm and reduces stuttering.

Instructions:
  1. Start with words like "el-e-phant" (3 syllables)
  2. Say each syllable clearly and separately
  3. Gradually speed up to normal pace
  4. Practice with different word lengths
  5. Focus on smooth transitions between syllables
MILD

Light Articulation

Use minimal tongue and lip pressure when forming sounds. This reduces muscle tension and improves speech clarity.

Instructions:
  1. Practice individual sounds: "ah", "ee", "oh"
  2. Hold each sound for 3-5 seconds
  3. Focus on relaxed jaw and lips
  4. Combine sounds into simple words
  5. Practice 5-10 minutes daily
MILD

Continuous Phonation

Practice maintaining a steady voice sound while saying vowels. This improves breath support and voice quality.

Instructions:
  1. Take a comfortable breath
  2. Say "ahhh" in a steady, continuous tone
  3. Focus on consistent volume and pitch
  4. Practice for 3-5 minutes daily
  5. Try different vowel sounds: "ohhh", "ehhh"
