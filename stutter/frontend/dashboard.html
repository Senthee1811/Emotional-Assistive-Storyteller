Breathing Exercises

Beginner
Diaphragmatic Breathing
Practice deep breathing from your diaphragm to improve speech flow and reduce tension.
Instructions:
  1. Sit comfortably with your back straight
  2. Place one hand on your chest and the other on your stomach
  3. Breathe in slowly through your nose for 4 seconds
  4. Hold your breath for 4 seconds
  5. Exhale slowly through your mouth for 6 seconds
  6. Repeat for 5-10 cycles

Pace Exercises

Intermediate
Syllable Timing
Practice speaking at a slow, steady pace by stretching out syllables.
Instructions:
  1. Choose a simple word like "hello" or "water"
  2. Break it into syllables: "hel-lo" or "wa-ter"
  3. Speak each syllable slowly, taking a breath between them
  4. Gradually increase speed while maintaining clarity
  5. Practice for 5-10 minutes daily

Voice Relaxation

Advanced
Gentle Onset
Learn to start words gently without hard attacks to reduce speech tension.
Instructions:
  1. Start with gentle "h" sound before words
  2. Practice with easy words like "hat", "car", "sun"
  3. Focus on smooth transitions between sounds
  4. Record yourself and listen for harsh glottal stops
  5. Gradually reduce gentle onset over time
